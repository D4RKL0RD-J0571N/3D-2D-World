## 1. Visual Direction & Mood

### The Tone
A collision of **grounded modern realism** and **untamed primeval fantasy**. The visual world is dark, mysterious, and organic, with bursts of ethereal luminescent flora and corrupted crystalline blight.

### The Contrast
- **The Modern Intruder (Jack)**: Matte olive-drabs, slate grays, weathered synthetic fabrics, angular stamped-metal supply crates, and utilitarian survival knives.
- **The Living Forest (The Realm)**: Ancient gnarled hardwoods, vibrant emerald mosses, deep loamy earth, turquoise mineral springs, and violent crimson veins of corruption.

## 2. Voxel Palette Standards

All voxel blocks are defined through `BlockDefinition` and categorized by `BlockRole`. Surfaces must adhere to standardized color values and texture densities to prevent visual clashing across greedy-meshed surfaces.

### 2.1 Color Keys & Materials

| Surface Type | Block ID | Role | Base Color / Hex | Surface Characteristic | Normal / Roughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Forest Loam (Dirt)** | `BLCK_0010` | `Floor` | Deep Earth Brown (`#2D1F14`) | Matte, rich soil with scattered root fragments | Low gloss, micro-roughness |
| **Lush Turf (Grass)** | `BLCK_0011` | `Floor` | Mossy Forest Green (`#1E3B20`) | Overgrown clover and fine moss top-layer | Soft diffuse, matte finish |
| **Ancient Stone (Cliff)** | `BLCK_0020` | `Wall` | Weathered Basalt Slate (`#383D42`) | Layered strata, vertical fracturing | Moderate specular, crisp edges |
| **Mossy Ruin Stone** | `BLCK_0021` | `Accent` | Lichen Gray-Green (`#3D4A3E`) | Flagstone tiles with creeping moss seams | Damp specular highlights |
| **Mineral Spring (Water)** | `BLCK_0030` | `Liquid` | Ethereal Cyan (`#1B4D5A`) | Translucent, gentle undulating surface | Smooth glassiness, planar refraction |
| **Blighted Soil** | `BLCK_0040` | `Floor` | Ash Violet / Crystalline (`#2A182E`) | Parched earth with glowing crimson fissures | Emissive red accents |

### 2.2 Texture & UV Tiling Guidelines
- **Greedy Meshing UV Rule**: Greedy meshing merges coplanar faces into large quads. Textures must tile seamlessly across merged UV coordinates:
  - Voxel UVs scale proportionally to quad dimensions (`Vector2(face.Size.x, face.Size.y)`).
  - Textures must use **Repeat** wrap mode.
  - Base texture resolution: $128 \times 128$ or $256 \times 256$ pixels per voxel meter to maintain a consistent texel density.

## 3. Billboarding & 2.5D Sprite Standards

Certain entities (such as minor fauna and minions) utilize 2.5D billboarded sprites to honor the "3D 2D World" heritage while maintaining high expressiveness.

### 3.1 Billboard Perspective Alignment
- Billboards utilize [`SpriteBillboard.cs`](file:///D:/Development/Game%20Development/3D%202D%20World/Assets/Scripts/Core/Utils/SpriteBillboard.cs).
- Sprites face the active camera along the Y-axis (cylindrical billboarding) to prevent unnatural tilting when the player looks up or down:
  ```csharp
  Vector3 lookDirection = camera.transform.position - transform.position;
  lookDirection.y = 0; // Lock vertical tilt
  transform.rotation = Quaternion.LookRotation(-lookDirection);
  ```

### 3.2 Squash-and-Stretch Animation
- Minions (like `SlimeEntity`) must communicate physics and intent through dynamic vertex/transform squash-and-stretch:
  - **Ascent / Jump**: Vertical elongation ($\text{Scale } Y = 1.25\times$, $\text{Scale } XZ = 0.89\times$).
  - **Impact / Landing**: Compression ($\text{Scale } Y = 0.70\times$, $\text{Scale } XZ = 1.19\times$).
  - **Volume Conservation Formula**: $\text{Scale}_{XZ} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\text{Scale}_Y}}$.

## 4. Conventional Prefab & Mesh Integration

To achieve a true hybrid look, conventional Unity 3D meshes (trees, foliage, crates, weapons) must blend organically with voxel geometry.

### 4.1 Grid Alignment & Snapping
- **Roots & Trunks**: Tree prefabs must have root flares that extend at least $0.5\,\text{m}$ below the pivot to sink naturally into uneven voxel steps.
- **Pivots**: All decorative prefabs must have their pivot centered at the bottom of the bounding volume (`(0, 0, 0)` at ground contact).
- **Socket Snapping**: Prefabs spawned via `DiscoverRoomSockets` instantiate at `Vector3(x + 0.5f, y, z + 0.5f)` to rest perfectly on voxel centers.

### 4.2 Collider Standards
- **Voxel Geometry**: Handled exclusively via `MeshCollider` generated by `GreedyMeshingService`.
- **Props (Crates, Barrels, Trees)**:
  - Solid obstacles use simple primitive colliders (`BoxCollider` or `CapsuleCollider`).
  - Never use complex `MeshCollider` on spawned prop prefabs to preserve physics performance.
- **Dynamic Actors**: Handled via `CharacterController` or trigger volumes.

## 5. Lighting, Atmosphere & Post-Processing (URP)

### 5.1 Lighting Setup
- **Directional Light (Canopy Sun)**:
  - Color: Pale Golden Amber (`#FFF1D0`), Intensity: $1.2$.
  - Soft shadows enabled.
  - Rotation: $50^\circ$ pitch, angled to cast long, dramatic shadows across clearings.
- **Ambient Light**:
  - Skybox ambient gradient: Deep Slate Navy sky (`#1C2430`), Moss Green equator (`#142218`), Dark Soil ground (`#0A0E0B`).

### 5.2 Atmosphere & Fog
- **URP Volumetric Fog**:
  - Exponential squared fog, Density: $0.025$.
  - Color: Deep Forest Teal (`#0E1F24`).
  - Distance: Start $5\,\text{m}$, fully obscures beyond $45\,\text{m}$ to maintain mystery and hide distant LODs.

### 5.3 Color Grading & Post-Processing
- **Tonemapping**: ACES for high dynamic range contrast.
- **Color Adjustments**:
  - Contrast: $+15\%$.
  - Saturation: $-10\%$ (overall desaturation, allowing emissive magic and crimson blight to pop intensely).
- **Bloom**:
  - Threshold: $0.85$, Intensity: $1.5$ (tuned for water reflections and bioluminescent flowers).
